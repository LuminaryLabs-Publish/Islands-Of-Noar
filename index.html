HOW TO PLAY

Rise above the clouds

Every floating island holds a tower. Follow the ascending ledges, time your jumps, and reach the glowing beacon on its summit. Press E to fly onward to the next island. The skyway crosses 20 islands and four biomes.

WASD move · Space jump · Mouse drag rotate camera · F enter or leave free flight · Space/C rise or descend while flying · R return to a checkpoint · N restart the journey
